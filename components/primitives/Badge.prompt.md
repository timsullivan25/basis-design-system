Status pill for machine-owned state. Uppercase-ish semibold micro-type; keep labels to one or two words.

```jsx
<Badge tone="positive" dot>Live</Badge>
<Badge tone="negative" icon="alert-triangle">Limit breach</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral" size="sm">T+1</Badge>
```

Tones map to semantic status tokens. `subtle={false}` for solid fills in dense tables where tint is lost.
